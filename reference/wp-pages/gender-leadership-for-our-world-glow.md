## GENDER LEADERSHIP FOR OUR WORLD - GLOW
HERI-Kenya’s Co-founder Professor Desireee LaBeaud, Stanford’s Associate Dean of Global Health, was selected for the prestigious WomenLift Health North Americam leadership journey. As part of this program, she started the GLOW (Gender Leadership for Our World) movement an intiative that uplifts women leaders across different sectors in Kenya by providing mentorship and opportunities to drive change in health & environmental spaces.
