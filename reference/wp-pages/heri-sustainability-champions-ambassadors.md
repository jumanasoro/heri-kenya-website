## HERI SUSTAINABILITY CHAMPIONS & AMBASSADORS
The HERI Global Ambassadors and Sustainability Champions initiative creates a pathway for individuals from diverse backgrounds to support sustainability without changing their daily lives or professions.
The initiative connects people who care about health, environment, and sustainability to practical action, resource mobilization, and advocacy aligned with grass roots actions.
## Why Ambassadors and Champions Matter
Many people want to contribute to sustainability but lack clear, credible ways to do so. At the same time, community-level initiatives often struggle to access networks, resources, and global visibility.
This initiative bridges that gap by enabling individuals to act as connectors, advocates, and supporters of locally grounded sustainability work.
It transforms goodwill and interest into meaningful contribution.
## Who Ambassadors and Champions Are
Ambassadors and Champions may include professionals, students, creatives, researchers, travelers, and community leaders.
They are united not by background, but by a shared commitment to advancing sustainability, health, and environmental responsibility.
Each participant contributes according to their capacity and context.
## How the Initiative Works
The Ambassadors and Champions initiative is voluntary and flexible, allowing individuals to engage in ways that align with their interests, capacities, and personal contexts.
Participants contribute to sustainability efforts through activities they are most comfortable with, such as awareness raising, resource mobilization, network building, storytelling, or advocacy. Engagement may take place at local, national, or global levels.
Rather than following a rigid structure, the initiative is guided by shared values of integrity, responsibility, and responsiveness to real community needs. This flexibility enables authentic participation while ensuring contributions support meaningful and locally grounded impact.
