From 6 to 8 June 2026, HERI-Kenya took part in the **3rd annual Diani Beach Zero Plastic Pollution Festival** at Kongo Beach, Diani, Kwale County.

The festival was hosted by SambaSports Youth Trust with partners including the Kenya Marine and Fisheries Research Institute (KMFRI) and the County Government of Kwale, bringing together organisations working to keep Diani's beaches and ocean free of plastic.
