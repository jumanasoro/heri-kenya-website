On 26 September 2026, HERI-Kenya joined the **Bahari Yetu Festival** at Regatta Beach, Galu, organised by COASTED with partners including WeWorld, Barnfonden and CRAWN Trust.

The festival's theme — *Our Ocean, Our Responsibility: Every Step Counts Against Plastic Pollution* — sits at the heart of our work on waste, plastics and coastal health. HERI-Kenya received a certificate of participation recognising our strong commitment to protecting the ocean and environment.
