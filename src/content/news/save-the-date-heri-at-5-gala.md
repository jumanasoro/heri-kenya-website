HERI-Kenya turns five this year, and we are marking the milestone with our **annual fundraising gala on Saturday, 12 December 2026**.

The evening brings together the communities we serve, our partners, supporters, board and members to celebrate five years of science-driven work for healthier environments and communities on Kenya's coast.

Funds raised will help us:

- Expand the Black Soldier Fly Farm's waste processing capacity
- Build a sustainability training and research hub
- Grow green livelihood programmes for youth and women
- Strengthen our capacity to reach more communities

Venue, time and ticket details will be announced soon. [Reserve your seat or sponsor the gala](/gala/#rsvp) — and if you can't attend, you can still [support HERI@5 online](https://give.socialgoodfund.org/52imR8?ref=ab_5sjm4pVIUtX5sjm4pVIUtX).
