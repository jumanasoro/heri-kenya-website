On 16 October 2026, HERI-Kenya received the **Awareness & Advocacy Excellence Award** at the **2nd Africa Circular Excellence Awards**, presented by WEEE Centre.

The awards were held as part of the **6th Africa International E-Waste Conference** at Diamonds Leisure Beach & Golf Resort, Diani, under the theme *From Policy to Practice: Scaling Africa's Circular Electronics Economy*.

The award recognises HERI's outstanding achievement in awareness and advocacy. Thank you to everyone — communities, partners, schools and volunteers — who makes this work possible.
