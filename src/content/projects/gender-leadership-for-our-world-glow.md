## About GLOW

HERI-Kenya co-founder **Prof. Desiree LaBeaud**, Stanford University’s Associate Dean of Global Health, was selected for the WomenLift Health North America leadership journey. As part of that programme she started **GLOW — Gender Leadership for Our World**, a movement that uplifts women leaders across sectors in Kenya.

GLOW provides mentorship and opportunities for women to drive change in health and environmental spaces.
