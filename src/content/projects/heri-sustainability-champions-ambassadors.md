## Why ambassadors and champions matter

Many people want to contribute to sustainability but lack a clear, credible way to do it. Community initiatives, meanwhile, often lack the networks, resources and visibility they need.

This initiative bridges that gap, letting individuals act as connectors, advocates and supporters of locally grounded work — turning goodwill into meaningful contribution.

## How it works

The programme is voluntary and flexible. Each person contributes according to their interests and capacity — locally, nationally or globally — guided by shared values of integrity, responsibility and responsiveness to real community needs.
