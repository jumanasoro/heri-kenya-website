## Why the Collective exists

Diani’s economy depends on healthy ecosystems, clean public spaces and resilient communities. Youth groups, women-led initiatives and community organisations already lead clean-ups, restoration and education — but many struggle to find steady funding, technical support and long-term platforms.

At the same time, hotels and tourism businesses want to invest in sustainability, yet without structure those investments are fragmented and hard to track. The Collective connects these needs through coordination, shared accountability and long-term planning.

## Focus areas

- Environmental restoration and conservation
- Organic waste management and circular economy solutions
- Public and environmental health awareness
- Community capacity building and skills
- Youth and women’s participation
- Applied research, learning and evidence

## Value for partners

Hotels and businesses gain structured engagement, measurable impact and stronger sustainability reporting — and help build Diani’s reputation as a responsible tourism destination. Donors and development partners gain one coordinated platform able to deliver integrated environmental, health and community outcomes.

Engagement is flexible, matching each partner’s priorities with local needs.
