## Why community forums matter

Many health and environmental problems persist not for lack of solutions, but because communities lack accurate information, trusted spaces for dialogue, and real chances to engage with experts and decision-makers.

Meanwhile, research and policy often stay disconnected from everyday life. HERI forums close that gap with inclusive, evidence-informed spaces where knowledge is shared, concerns are heard and solutions are shaped together.

## What the forums address

- Public and environmental health risks
- Organic waste management and circular economy practices
- Climate and environmental resilience
- Livelihoods and sustainability skills
- Youth and women’s participation in local solutions
- Research findings and what they mean for policy

Topics are chosen for local relevance and emerging needs.

## What communities gain

Participants leave with clarity on the risks they face, knowledge of practical solutions and the confidence to adopt them at home — and forums strengthen social cohesion by building shared responsibility for the places people live.
