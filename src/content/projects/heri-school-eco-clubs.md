## Why eco-clubs matter

Upper primary school is when values, curiosity and career interests take shape. Sustainability appears in the curriculum, but many pupils never get to experience it in a practical, engaging way.

Eco-clubs close that gap — turning classroom knowledge into hands-on activities that make learning tangible, relevant and memorable.

## What the clubs focus on

- Environmental and public health awareness
- Organic waste management and responsible behaviour
- Climate and environmental resilience
- Biodiversity and ecosystem protection
- Leadership, teamwork and problem-solving

## Mentorship and career exposure

Sessions are led by carefully selected mentors from health, environmental and sustainability fields. They are role models who show pupils how sustainability works in the real world — helping them see it as both a responsibility and a possible future profession.
