## Restoring Diani, together

HERI mobilises local communities, schools and volunteers for ongoing tree planting and coastal clean-ups. These activities restore natural habitats and reduce pollution — and they unite people in collective action against climate change while protecting Diani’s unique ecosystems.

The result is cleaner, greener and healthier spaces for both people and wildlife, and stronger environmental resilience for the whole community.
